FAQs

Why do our clients choose us?
How do we deliver?

We combine local pickup partners with dedicated land, air and sea routes so your shipment stays visible from pickup to delivery.

Do you provide shipping insurance?

We offer insurance, but the final decision is up to you. The insurance covers 1% of the value of the goods when delivered by sea, and 2% when it’s delivered by air or by air. Some goods have special, higher prices for insurance. Such goods include laptops, smartphones, and other electronics. We also only insure goods when the goods have been packed properly and in full.

How do we check the goods?

Our teams verify package condition, labels and dimensions before dispatch to help prevent delays.

How do we pack the goods?

We recommend durable outer packaging, internal cushioning and clear handling labels. Our team can also advise on special cargo.

How to get the cargo?

Choose pickup or delivery during your quote request and we will coordinate the handoff with you.

How to calculate all expenses?

Use the calculator for an estimate, then contact us for duties, insurance and route-specific fees.
